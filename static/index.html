DATA GOVERNANCE • ENTERPRISE COMPLIANCE

Privacy Policy

1. Information Collection & Processing

Nexus-Flow collects procurement specifications, electrical requirements, and quantity parameters entered into the workspace. When authenticating, credentials are cryptographically protected via salted PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 with 100,000 rounds. We do not store payment card numbers; all payment transactions are processed securely through the PayPal Commerce Platform.

2. Sourcing and Third-Party API Integrations

Procurement queries transmitted to Channel3 and Kernel are strictly anonymized technical component strings. No buyer identity or financial account information is provided to supplier catalog endpoints.

3. PayPal Payment Data Governance

PayPal Orders v2 transactions are governed by PayPal's privacy framework. Nexus-Flow maintains order identifiers, capture references, and settlement timestamps to guarantee multi-vendor escrow delivery verification.
